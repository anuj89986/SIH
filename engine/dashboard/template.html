Polar station fuel-survivability console

What this is

The two constraints below exist at a polar station and at no grid-connected site. They are the product; everything else is table stakes.

Annual resupply. The tank is filled once a year. The objective is not minimum cost per kWh but maximum probability of serving critical load until the ship returns. A Monte Carlo allocator over historical weather years sets a daily litre allowance, and the optimiser pays a steep price for every litre beyond it, with carry-over between horizons.
Science integrity. Locally emitted exhaust is unrepresentative of the region and corrupts the station's own atmospheric record. When forecast wind would carry the plume to the sampling inlet, generator operation is priced -- battery and renewables carry the clean-air window.
Physical availability. Forecast weather is not forecast generation: drifted panels and iced blades decorrelate the two. Availability is modelled as a state variable and learned as a forecast target.
